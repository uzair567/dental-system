A clinic built around unhurried care

Meridian Dental opened in 2012 with a simple idea: dental visits go better when nobody feels rushed. That's still how we run every appointment today.

Our approach

We block real time for every appointment, explain findings in plain language before recommending anything, and always walk you through cost up front. Nothing gets added to a treatment plan without your sign-off.

Our two resident dentists work alongside a small, steady front-desk and assisting team, so the person who greets you at the door usually already knows your name.

What guides us

Honest treatment plans

We recommend what your teeth need — not what fills a schedule.

Comfort first

Numbing, pacing, and check-ins are adjusted to how you're doing, every visit.

Clear records

Your history, X-rays and treatment notes stay organised and accessible to you on request.

Inside the clinic

Two treatment rooms, a dedicated X-ray room, and a waiting area designed to not feel like one.
